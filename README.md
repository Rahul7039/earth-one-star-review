# One Star, Five Stars for the Food

An alien tourist's one-star review of planet Earth — that still slips in a real compliment about the food. One sentence, 166 characters.

> "One star — the dosa was glorious enough to delay my invasion, but your species charges money for water that falls free from the sky, so I am leaving a review instead."

## Why this line

The first build led with the tired "came to conquer" opener and ended on a flat "left because". The revision keeps the strongest grievance — humans charging money for water that falls free from the sky — and the dosa compliment, but folds the invasion into a punchline (the food was glorious enough to delay it) and turns the review itself into the alien's retaliation: leaving a review instead.

## Run it

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the review, byte-identical, to `/output/entry.txt`.
